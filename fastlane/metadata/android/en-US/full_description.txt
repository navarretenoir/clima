Clima is a weather app for Chile. It shows current weather, the next 24 hours and 10 days for any of Chile's 346 communes, with an animated sky for every condition, day and night. Includes PM2.5 air alerts, driving restrictions, official school-closure notices, moon phase and tides. Data: Open-Meteo. No ads, no trackers, no accounts. Android 5.0+. Developed by Javiera Navarrete. (Interface in Spanish.)
